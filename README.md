# Lafz

> Word-by-word animated captions for your videos. Free, and fully offline.

Lafz listens to your video, writes down every word with its exact timing, and puts animated captions on the video. It works in Urdu, Hindi, English, Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi, Bengali, Tamil and other languages, and handles videos that switch between languages. Everything runs on your own computer: your videos never leave it.

## Features

- **Speech to captions on your computer.** OpenAI Whisper (whisper.cpp), with the model that suits your computer, chosen in Setup.
- **Urdu in Urdu script**, Hindi in Devanagari, English, and mixed-language speech. **Song mode** for music videos.
- **50 caption styles and 42 templates:** karaoke highlight, pop, neon, typewriter, cinematic, Urdu Nastaliq and more. Each caption can have its own look.
- **Edit everything:** words, timing, line layout (one, two or more lines), position, size, colours and entrance direction.
- **Export** the finished video (MP4), captions only on a transparent background (ProRes 4444, for your editor), or subtitle files (SRT, VTT, TXT).
- **Private:** no account and no upload. The internet is only used once, in Setup, to download the speech model.

## Install

1. Download **[Lafz-Setup.exe](https://github.com/conect2hamza/lafz-releases/releases/latest/download/Lafz-Setup.exe)** and open it.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is because Lafz is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate; it does not mean anything was found. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
   Your browser may also say the file is "not commonly downloaded": choose **Keep**.
3. Follow the installer. On first start, Lafz checks your computer and downloads the speech model that suits it (about 0.6–1.6 GB, once). No internet? Choose **Import from file** in Setup and give it a model file.

**Needs:** Windows 10 (1809) or 11, 64-bit, and about 3 GB of free disk space.

## Updates and uninstall

Download the newest **Lafz-Setup.exe** and install it over the old version; your projects stay. To remove Lafz: Windows Settings → Apps → Lafz → Uninstall.
